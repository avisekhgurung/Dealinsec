# Where things are (routes)

- /dashboard — KPIs, trial countdown, deal status, recent deals
- /deals — pipeline list · /deals/new — create deal · /deals/:id — deal details (quote/agreement actions live here)
- /deals/:id/quote — quotation document view
- /quotations — quotations register
- /contracts — agreements list · /contracts/:id — agreement details (invoice actions) · /contracts/:id/export — print/PDF view
- /invoices — invoices list · /brand-invoices/:id — invoice document (mark paid, attachments)
- /profile — personal + business info, bank details, signature (Account-setup stepper; /profile?edit=1&section=bank jumps straight to a section)
- /settings — Organization, Team Members (invite, roles & permission matrix), Activity Logs, Subscription, Preferences (theme)
- /pricing — plans & billing (in-app)
Sidebar on desktop (collapse with [), bottom navigation on mobile.
