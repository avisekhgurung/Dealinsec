# Terminology

- Prospect: a company Client Radar found and checked; not yet in your pipeline.
- Client Radar: the prospect research feature (/outbound): searches, prospects, outreach and (where Gmail is on) the inbox.
- Lead: a company you are pursuing, with a stage, next steps, facts and messages.
- Do-not-contact list: addresses and domains DealInSec will never let you message.
- Deal: one client engagement (client, engagement model, service, value, dates, deliverables).
- Engagement model: how a deal is billed — retainer, fixed price, hourly, milestones, subscription and so on.
- Brand deal: a Brand Collaboration deal — a creator's paid work for a brand (a secondary use).
- Work type: the account setting (Settings → Work type) that chooses client or
  brand wording; it does not change existing deals or documents.
- Quotation/Quote: the priced proposal document generated from a deal.
- Agreement (also "contract" in URLs): the electronic agreement created from a
  deal; one per deal; Signed once the client signs online or a signed copy is uploaded.
- Invoice: bill raised from an agreement; can be split (advance/final).
- Deal Credit: free-plan allowance; 1 credit = 1 deal + its quotation, per org per month.
- Deal Boost: a retired add-on that is no longer sold. Accounts that bought one
  earlier keep unlimited deals & quotations until it expires. For more, suggest Pro.
- Organization: the account for your business; teammates join it with a role.
- Owner: the member who holds billing; the plan lives on the owner.
- Trial: the 7-day all-Pro period every new account starts with.
