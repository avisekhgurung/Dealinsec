# Terminology

- Deal: one client engagement (client name, title, amount, dates, deliverables).
- Quotation/Quote: the priced proposal document generated from a deal.
- Agreement (also "contract" in URLs): the electronic agreement created from a
  deal; one per deal; Signed once proof is uploaded.
- Invoice: bill raised from an agreement; can be split (advance/final).
- Deal Credit: free-plan allowance; 1 credit = 1 deal + its quotation; 4/month per org.
- Deal Boost: a retired add-on that is no longer sold. Accounts that bought one
  earlier keep unlimited deals & quotations until it expires; it never
  unlocked agreements, invoices or payment tracking. For more, suggest Pro.
- Organization: the account for your business — usually just you; anyone you
  add joins as a member with a role.
- Owner: the member who holds billing; the plan lives on the owner.
- Trial: the 7-day all-Pro period every new account starts with.
