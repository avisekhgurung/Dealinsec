# Common questions

**Who is DealInSec for?** Founder-led B2B service businesses of about 1 to 10
people — digital marketing, SEO, growth, AI automation, web and software
development, content and lead-generation agencies, and independent B2B
consultants — in the United States, other international markets and India.
Freelancers use it the same way, and creators use it for paid brand
collaborations.
**How can it help my SEO (or marketing, or AI automation) agency find
clients?** AI Outbound researches companies that match the clients you want
— for example "US SaaS companies with 20–200 employees that need SEO" — and
shows, for each, what its own website says, recent dated reasons to reach out
and the people to contact, with sources. You add the good ones to Leads,
approve an AI-drafted first message and send it from your own email. Then the
same workspace carries the won deal to quotation, agreement, invoice and payment.
**Is DealInSec free?** Yes to start — a 7-day all-Pro trial (no card), then a
permanent free plan. Paid plans can only be bought in India for now; see
product-facts for the prices and limits.
**Does DealInSec find clients for me?** It researches them with you: you
describe who you want, confirm the profile, and it finds matching companies,
checks each against the profile and shows the evidence (source and date),
labelling guesses and unknowns. You decide who goes into Leads. Research has
daily limits per workspace; contact details aren't always available.
**Does DealInSec send emails for me?** No. AI drafts a first message, you
approve the exact text, send it from your own email and mark it sent; a
follow-up task is then added. The do-not-contact list is checked first.
Nothing — first messages, follow-ups or payment reminders — is sent
automatically.
**Does it work with Gmail?** Connecting Gmail is in limited early access for
selected workspaces while Google's verification is completed, so it isn't
available to everyone yet. Where it is on, replies to your outreach appear in
an inbox, sorted by fixed rules (never by AI — Gmail content is never sent to
an AI model), and a reply, opt-out or bounce stops pending follow-ups.
**Can I turn a prospect into a quotation and an invoice?** Yes: add the
prospect to Leads, turn the won lead into a deal, then generate the
quotation, the agreement (Pro) and the invoices (Pro) from that deal.
**Can I use DealInSec from the US or India?** Yes, from either, and from most
countries: the workspace uses your country's currency and document
conventions. Buying a paid plan currently works only in India; everywhere
else the free plan and the 7-day trial are open.
**Which payment methods are supported?** For buying DealInSec in India:
Razorpay with UPI, cards and netbanking, in rupees. DealInSec does not
collect payments from your clients — they pay you directly and you record it.
**Will it make my client pay?** Nothing can force an unwilling client to pay.
DealInSec gets scope and payment terms agreed in writing before you start,
gets the invoice out on time, drafts the follow-up, and keeps a dated record
of what the client accepted if they dispute it later.
**Can I add a teammate?** Yes — invite them in Settings → Team Members and
control what they can do with roles (Pro and the trial include 5 members).
**What is an agreement here?** A professionally worded electronic agreement
generated from your deal, with your details and signature image applied, that
your client signs online through a link (or on paper). It is electronic
acceptance with an audit record, not a certified digital signature. For
questions about legal enforceability, consult a lawyer — DealInSec provides
the workflow and records, not legal advice.
**Does it handle GST or sales tax?** Your registration numbers (PAN and GSTIN
in India, a VAT number in the UK and EU) print on your documents, but app
invoices don't calculate tax and aren't GST tax invoices. For an Indian GST
tax invoice use the free tool at /tools/gst-invoice-generator; your
accountant can confirm what you need.
**Does it replace my CRM or Salesforce?** It's a lighter, connected
workspace for a small team's own pipeline and paperwork — it doesn't
integrate with other CRMs, run email campaigns or act as an autonomous sales agent.
**Where do I change theme?** Settings → Preferences (Light/Dark/System).
**How do I reset my password?** Sign-in page → "Forgot password?" — the email
link is valid for 30 minutes.
**Can I edit a deal after creating the agreement?** No — deals lock once they
leave Pending. Amounts and terms are frozen into the workflow.
