# Common questions

**Who is DealInSec for?** Founder-led agencies and consultants with one to a
handful of people (SEO, digital marketing, growth, B2B marketing and
AI-automation agencies) in the United States, India and elsewhere. Freelancers
use it the same way, and creators use it for paid brand collaborations.
**Is DealInSec free?** Yes to start — a 7-day all-Pro trial (no card), then a
free plan with 4 deals/month. Pro is ₹99/month or ₹999/year, and can only be
bought in India for now; outside India (including the US) the free plan and the
trial are open, but paid plans can't be bought yet.
**Does DealInSec find clients for me?** AI Outbound helps you research them:
describe who you want, confirm the profile, and it researches matching
companies, checks each against the profile and shows the evidence (source and
date), labelling guesses and unknowns. You decide who goes into Leads. Research
has daily limits per workspace; contact details aren't always available.
**Does DealInSec send emails for me?** No. AI drafts a first message, you
approve the exact text, send it from your own email and mark it sent; a
follow-up task is then added. The do-not-contact list is checked first.
Sending through a connected Gmail account isn't available yet.
**Will it make my client pay?** Nothing can force an unwilling client to pay.
DealInSec gets scope and payment terms agreed in writing before you start,
gets the invoice out on time, drafts the follow-up, and keeps a dated record
of what the client accepted if they dispute it later.
**Does DealInSec collect payments from my clients?** No. You record payments
against invoices; DealInSec never touches client money.
**Can I add an assistant or partner?** Yes — invite them in Settings → Team
Members and control what they can do with roles.
**What is an agreement here?** A professionally-worded electronic agreement
generated from your deal, with your details and signature image applied, that
you and your client execute. It is electronic acceptance with an audit record,
not a Digital Signature Certificate or Aadhaar eSign. For questions about
legal enforceability, consult a lawyer — DealInSec provides the workflow and
records, not legal advice.
**Does it handle GST?** Your PAN and GSTIN print on your documents, but app
invoices don't calculate GST and aren't Rule-46 tax invoices. For a GST tax
invoice use the free tool at /tools/gst-invoice; your CA can confirm what you need.
**Where do I change theme?** Settings → Preferences (Light/Dark/System).
**How do I reset my password?** Sign-in page → "Forgot password?" — the email
link is valid for 30 minutes.
**Can I edit a deal after creating the agreement?** No — deals lock once they
leave Pending. Amounts and terms are frozen into the workflow.
