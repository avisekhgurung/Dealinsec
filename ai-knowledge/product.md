# What DealInSec is

DealInSec is a client-acquisition and deal-to-payment workspace for
freelancers, consultants and small B2B service agencies: AI-assisted prospect
research, leads, deals, quotations, agreements, invoices and payment tracking
in one place. Find Clients. Close Deals. Get Paid.

Who it is for: freelancers, solo consultants and small B2B service agencies of
about 1 to 10 people, including web development and software services,
digital marketing, SEO, growth, AI automation, content and lead-generation
businesses. It is launching first in India, and Indian businesses can find
clients in India and abroad. Creators can use it for paid brand
collaborations, but they are not the main audience.

What it does not do: it does not guarantee clients, replies, meetings or
sales; it is not a verified contact database (contact details are not always
found); it is not an autonomous sales agent and never sends messages on its
own; and it does not replace accounting software.

The problem it solves: a small agency's sales live in too many places —
prospect lists in a spreadsheet, outreach in Gmail, deals in someone's head,
quotations and agreements in documents, invoices in another tool, and who
has paid in a bank app. Follow-ups slip, scope is agreed verbally, and
invoices go out late. DealInSec keeps it in one connected workflow:

**Prospect → Lead → Deal → Quotation → Agreement → Invoice → Payment tracking.**

1. AI Outbound researches companies that match who you want to reach and
   shows the evidence for each (source and date). You decide who becomes a lead.
2. Leads move through a pipeline with next steps, follow-up dates and a
   do-not-contact list. AI drafts a first message; you approve the exact text
   and send it from your own email.
3. A won lead becomes a deal with its engagement model (retainer, fixed
   price, hourly, milestones and so on), value and deliverables.
4. The deal produces a quotation the client can accept online, then an
   agreement the client can sign online, then invoices, then payment tracking.

What the agreement contains: Parties, Scope of Work, Deliverables & Timeline,
Compensation, an ownership clause, Exclusivity, and Governing Law that
follows the country the workspace works from (Indian workspaces cite the
Indian Contract Act 1872; elsewhere no statute is named). Ownership of the
final deliverables passes to the client on full payment. Optional standard
terms: 50% advance, balance within 7 days of delivery, 2 revision rounds,
advance non-refundable on cancellation.

What it cannot do: force an unwilling client to pay, recover money, collect
payments, or send email on its own. It gets scope and payment terms agreed in
writing before the work starts, gets invoices out on time, and keeps a dated
record of what the client accepted.

Key facts:
- Money follows the workspace's country and currency (50 currencies). Indian
  workspaces print PAN and GSTIN; UK and EU workspaces print a VAT number.
- DealInSec records and tracks payments — it NEVER moves or collects client money.
- Every account belongs to an Organization (your business); teammates join it with roles.
- Web app + installable PWA; works on mobile and desktop; light and dark theme (Settings → Preferences).
