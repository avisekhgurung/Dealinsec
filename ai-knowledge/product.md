# What DealInSec is

DealInSec is deal-management software for India's deal-led service businesses:
real estate consultants/brokers, interior designers, architects, marketing &
digital agencies, and construction contractors.

One workflow: **Deal → Quotation → Agreement → Invoice → Payment tracking.**
You look professional to clients, get every deal in writing, and never lose
track of who owes you.

Key facts:
- Made for India: ₹ amounts, GST-oriented invoicing fields on profile (PAN, GSTIN), sector-specific agreement copy (RERA, drawings-IP, defect liability etc.).
- DealInSec records and tracks payments — it NEVER moves or collects client money.
- Every account belongs to an Organization; teammates join with roles and permissions.
- Web app + installable PWA; works on mobile and desktop; light and dark theme (Settings → Preferences).
