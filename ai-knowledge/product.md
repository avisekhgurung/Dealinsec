# What DealInSec is

DealInSec is deal-management software for India's freelancers — designers,
developers, writers, video editors & photographers, marketers and consultants.
Solo independent professionals who quote, sign and bill their own clients.

One workflow: **Deal → Quotation → Agreement → Invoice → Payment tracking.**
The problem: you do the work, and the client pays late, pays less, or never
pays. Most of that starts with disorganisation — no written scope, an invoice
sent late or never, awkward chasing, scope creep. DealInSec gets scope and
payment terms agreed in writing before you start, keeps the invoice and
follow-up on the same deal, and leaves a dated record of what the client
accepted if they later dispute it. What it cannot do: force an unwilling client
to pay, or recover money for you. There is no guarantee of payment.

What the agreement contains: Parties, Scope of Work, Deliverables & Timeline,
Compensation, an ownership clause, Exclusivity (exclusive or non-exclusive)
and Governing Law (Indian Contract Act 1872). For freelance work the ownership
clause says rights in the final deliverables pass to the client only on full
and final payment — until then the work stays yours — and you may show it in
your portfolio unless agreed otherwise. Consulting deals get a Deliverables &
Confidentiality clause instead (advice in good faith, no guaranteed outcome).
Optional standard terms: 50% advance, balance within 7 days of delivery, 2
revision rounds, advance non-refundable on cancellation.

Key facts:
- Made for India: ₹ amounts, PAN and GSTIN from your profile printed on your documents.
- DealInSec records and tracks payments — it NEVER moves or collects client money.
- Every account belongs to an Organization (your business — for most freelancers, just you).
- Web app + installable PWA; works on mobile and desktop; light and dark theme (Settings → Preferences).
